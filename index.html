03 / Reading the map

Connections,
with context.

Orion Weave supports literature discovery and research analysis. Its visual relationships are tools for exploration, with a clear distinction between classification, sample evidence, and source search counts.

What does a connection mean?

Dashed links represent the project's topic taxonomy. Solid links require direct evidence for both topics in the same paper record, and count distinct works conservatively. Click a solid link to inspect supporting metadata. These are thematic relationships, not citations, causality, verified method adoption, or full-text review.

Which papers contribute to a count?

The public demo counts only arXiv samples with direct metadata support, plus their navigational ancestor memberships. Uncertain, rejected, comparison-only, and excluded records do not contribute independent relationships. Topic counts can overlap and should not be summed as unique publications. The full local edition additionally retains source-reported query totals for comparison.

What does an empty circle mean?

A small hollow marker preserves a category's place in the hierarchy when no matching records are present under the current basis and filters. In sample mode, zero means no matching collected samples; it does not establish that no such papers exist.

What is included in the public version?

The public version has no file upload, export, API-key entry, account, or live data-collection functions. It serves a fixed arXiv metadata snapshot and runs interactions in your browser. The full local edition retains collection tools, complete research records, and import/export features. Displayed web content can still be saved by a browser; removing export controls is not copy protection.
